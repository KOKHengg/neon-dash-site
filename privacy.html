Privacy Policy

Neon Dash: Tunnel Run · Effective 20 June 2026
Short version: Neon Dash: Tunnel Run does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data, and it does not transmit any data about you to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, your progress and preferences — such as your high score, unlocked characters and skins, mission progress, energy, and sound/haptics settings — are saved locally on your device only (using standard on-device storage). This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or any tracking technologies.

In-app purchases

This version of the app does not offer in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a future update, they will be processed by Apple through the App Store, and Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, no personal information from children is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
