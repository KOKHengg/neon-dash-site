Privacy Policy

Neon Dash: Tunnel Runner 3D · Effective 1 October 2026
Short version: Neon Dash does not collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. If you buy an optional in-app item, a record of that purchase is processed by our payment partner, RevenueCat, using an anonymous ID that is not linked to your identity.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment card details. The only data that leaves your device is purchase history, and only if you make an in-app purchase (see below).

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, your progress and preferences — such as your high score, unlocked characters and skins, mission progress, energy, and sound/haptics settings — are saved locally on your device only (using standard on-device storage). This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (energy packs and a one-time Starter Bundle). Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of the transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

To verify purchases, prevent fraud, and restore your Starter Bundle, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. When the app opens or you buy something, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, the transaction details from Apple (which item, price, currency, and date), and basic technical information such as your App Store country, app version, and device type. This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to make purchases work (App Functionality) and to show us aggregate sales figures (Analytics).

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app contains no advertising and no tracking. We do not use cookies, the advertising identifier (IDFA), or any technology that follows you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. In-app purchases on a child's device are controlled by the parent's Apple settings (such as Ask to Buy and Screen Time).

Data retention, deletion, and your choices

We have no account system and do not store personal data on our own servers. Anonymous purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to verify and restore purchases and for our sales records. Game progress is erased by deleting the app from your device. To ask us to delete the anonymous purchase record for your device, or for any other question about your data, email kokheng0508@gmail.com and we will respond.

Third parties

The only third parties involved are Apple (App Store payments) and RevenueCat (purchase verification), as described above. There are no advertising networks or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
